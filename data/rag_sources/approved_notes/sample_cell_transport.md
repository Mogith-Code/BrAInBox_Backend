# Unit 2 Chemical and Cellular Basis of Life

## Cell membrane and transport

The cell membrane is a fluid mosaic of phospholipids and proteins. It is selectively permeable,
so it controls which substances enter and leave the cell.

### Passive transport

Diffusion is the net movement of particles from a region of higher concentration to a region of
lower concentration. Osmosis is the diffusion of water molecules across a selectively permeable
membrane. Facilitated diffusion uses channel or carrier proteins but needs no energy from the cell.

### Active transport

Active transport moves ions or molecules against their concentration gradient. It needs energy
from ATP and carrier proteins called pumps. The sodium-potassium pump moves three sodium ions out
of the cell and two potassium ions into the cell for every ATP used. Root hair cells take up
mineral ions from the soil by active transport.

### Bulk transport

Endocytosis takes large particles into the cell in vesicles; exocytosis releases substances such
as enzymes and hormones out of the cell.
